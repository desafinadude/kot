# 🎨 Art Generation Brief — Monster City: Kaiju Edition

**Task:** Generate game art as **contact sheets** (one image containing all items in a
grid — much faster than generating individually). Produce the **six sheets** below
(in any order). Output as PNG, exactly at the given dimensions.

---

## Art style (all sheets)

- **Modern comic-book style** — bold confident ink outlines with varying line weight,
  flat cel-shaded colors with 2–3 tone steps, subtle **halftone dot** shading, a little
  manga dynamism in the pose/energy, but **not** full manga (no black-and-white screentone,
  no anime-school look). Think **kaiju comic-cover art**: menacing, punchy, fun.
- **Palette:** sumi-ink outlines `#2b2118`, flat background **parchment `#f0e6d2`**
  (aged paper), vermilion accent `#b23a2e`. Use each character's color (given below).
- **Hard rules:** flat parchment background in every cell · **NO text, letters, labels,
  borders, grid lines or watermarks** — numerals allowed ONLY on the dice sheet (Sheet 2),
  exactly as listed · nothing cropped — keep ~8% margin inside each cell ·
  each item **centered in its cell**.

## Sheet 2 — The dice: 6 black + 2 green (6 × 2 contact sheet)

The game uses **8 dice (6 black + 2 green)** — one shared six-face
design in two colorways. **Make them solid: the whole die is its color, with the
symbols painted on it** — not an outline, not an inset panel.

- **Dimensions: 1536 × 1024 px** — **6 columns × 2 rows = 12 cells**,
  cell exactly **256 × 512 px** (1536÷6 = 256, 1024÷2 = 512).
- **Columns (identical in both rows, left → right):** `1 · 2 · 3 · ⚡ · 🐾 · ♥`
- **Row 1 — the 6 black dice:** **solid sumi-black `#2b2118` bodies.** Every symbol
  (numerals and icons) painted directly on the black in **parchment `#f0e6d2`** —
  bold comic strokes, high contrast, no other colors.
- **Row 2 — the green dice:** **solid matcha-green `#5c7d4a` bodies**, same six
  faces, symbols also parchment `#f0e6d2`. (The set has 2 green dice sharing this
  face set — drawing all six faces covers every face needed.)
- Each die is a **square drawn centered in its cell** (parchment margins around it),
  comic treatment: thick outline, slight bevel, light halftone corners.

## Output & next steps

1. Save each sheet as PNG at 1536 × 1024: `sheet_characters.png`, `sheet_dice.png`,
   `sheet_cards_3.png` … `sheet_cards_6.png`.
2. Hand them back to the dev assistant for slicing:
   - **Characters (Sheet 1, 3 × 2):** 512 px cells → `assets/art/monsters/*.webp`
     (order: `ryuga, shirohime, tetsujin9, onizaru, kagero, ganryu`).
   - **Dice (Sheet 2, 6 × 2):** cell 256 × 512 → x = 0/256/512/768/1024/1280,
     y = 0 (row 1) and y = 512 (row 2). Columns are 1, 2, 3, ⚡, 🐾, ♥;
     row 1 = black, row 2 = green →
     `assets/art/dice/{1,2,3,energy,attack,heal}_{black|green}.png`.
   - **Cards (Sheets 3–6, 6 × 4):** 256 px cells → `assets/art/cards/<card-id>.png`
     (e.g. `gas-refinery.png`), wired into `data/art.json` as card thumbnails.
3. If a sheet comes back with stray text, wrong grid, or clipped items — just
   regenerate that sheet; contact sheets are cheap.

_Generated art replaces the kanji-swatch placeholders automatically via `data/art.json`._
